# AHIIA.AI Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026\
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information AHIIA.AI ("we", "us", "our") collects, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website at https://ahiia.ai, our platform, apps, AI tools and related services (the "Services").

This policy is part of our Terms of Service. By using AHIIA.AI, you confirm that you have read and understood it.

## 2. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the Services well. Depending on how you use AHIIA.AI, that may include:

### 2.1 Information you give us

- **Account details:** your name, email address, password and any business or company details you add when you register.
- **Content you submit:** the prompts, documents, business information, financial figures, client details and files you enter or upload to generate or manage documents.
- **Payment information:** billing details needed to process your subscription. Card payments are handled by our payment providers, so we do not store your full card details.
- **Support messages:** anything you send us when you contact our support team.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data:** the features you use, pages you visit, documents you create and how often you use the platform.
- **Device and technical data:** your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language and approximate location based on your IP address.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** small files that help us keep you signed in, remember your settings and understand how the platform is used (see section 8).

### 2.3 Information from third parties

We may receive limited information from payment processors, sign-in providers or analytics tools, for example confirmation that a payment succeeded.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

1. Create and manage your account.
2. Provide the Services, including generating, storing and exporting your documents.
3. Process payments, renewals and refunds.
4. Respond to your questions and give you support.
5. Improve, test and secure the platform, and fix problems.
6. Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
7. Send you important service messages, such as account notices, billing updates and changes to our policies.
8. Send you product updates or offers, where you have agreed to receive them or where the law allows. You can opt out at any time.
9. Comply with our legal obligations.

## 4. How AI is involved

AHIIA.AI uses AI to generate and improve documents. When you ask the AI to do something, the content you submit is processed by our systems and by AI service providers working on our behalf so that we can return your result.

We only use your submitted content as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain your account, keep the platform secure, give support and meet legal obligations. As explained in our Terms, you keep ownership of your content.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information (such as government ID numbers, health information or passwords) into prompts or documents unless it is truly needed. Also make sure you have the right to share any third party's information that you upload.

## 5. Our legal basis for using your information

We process personal information under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable data protection laws. Depending on the situation, we rely on:

- **Contract:** we need your information to provide the Services you signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests:** such as keeping our platform secure, preventing fraud and improving our Services, where these interests are not outweighed by your rights.
- **Consent:** for example, for certain cookies or marketing messages. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where we must keep or share information to comply with the law.

## 6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed, and only with:

- **Service providers** that help us run AHIIA.AI, such as cloud hosting and infrastructure providers, AI providers, payment processors, analytics tools and customer support tools. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
- **Professional advisers**, such as lawyers, accountants and auditors, where necessary.
- **Authorities and regulators**, where we are required by law, court order or legal process, or where we need to protect our rights, users or the public.
- **A buyer or successor**, if AHIIA.AI is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. We would tell you if your information became subject to a different privacy policy.

These third parties have their own terms and privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they operate independently of us.

## 7. International transfers

Our service providers may be located outside Nigeria, so your information may be processed in other countries. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected and that transfers follow applicable data protection law.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies to:

- Keep you signed in and secure your account.
- Remember your preferences.
- Understand how people use the platform so we can improve it.

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. If you disable essential cookies, parts of AHIIA.AI may not work properly.

## 9. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Services. After that, we keep it only as long as necessary to meet legal, tax, accounting or security requirements, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When we no longer need it, we delete or anonymise it.

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are legally required to keep certain records.

## 10. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. These include access controls, encrypted connections and monitoring of our systems.

No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can help by choosing a strong password, keeping it private, and telling us immediately if you suspect your account has been accessed without your permission.

## 11. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Delete** your information, in certain circumstances.
- **Object to or restrict** certain types of processing.
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on it, without affecting what we did before you withdrew.
- **Receive a copy** of your information in a commonly used format, where applicable.
- **Opt out** of marketing messages at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
- **Complain** to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or the data protection authority in your country, if you believe your rights have been breached.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at consultahiiaai@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the time required by law.

## 12. Children's privacy

AHIIA.AI is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use it, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.
